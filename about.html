How to play World Word

World Word is a global word frequency game and a social word game alternative for fans of Wordle-style feedback. Submit any valid word, see it on the live leaderboard, and share your rank with friends.

Rules

  1. Enter one English word (A–Z only, max 50 letters) and tap Submit Word.
  2. The game checks the dictionary, then shows animated tiles with times said and global rank.
  3. The top 50 words worldwide appear on the leaderboard, updating every 30 seconds or when you refresh.
  4. Use Share on your result to post emoji tiles, rank, and a link with UTM tracking.

Daily challenge & streaks

There is no single “word of the day” to guess. You can still play it as a daily word game: submit once per calendar day and build a streak shown on your result card. Try to pick words that climb the board — or niche words only you would think of.

FAQ

Is this a Wordle clone?

No — it is inspired by Wordle’s tile aesthetic but focused on guess the word-free competition: you choose the word, the planet ranks it.

Who made World Word?

World Word was created by Ezequiel Casillas and runs on Cloudflare at worldwordgame.com.

Is it free?

Yes. No account required — a visitor ID in your browser helps highlight your word on the board.

How does ranking work?

Rank is by how many times a word has been submitted globally. Ties follow server ordering; refresh the leaderboard to see the latest counts.
