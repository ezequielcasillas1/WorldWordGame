HOW TO PLAY

World Word is a real-time global word frequency experiment. Every word submitted joins a single shared pool across the planet.

W O R L D
  • 1. Type Any Single Word: Enter a single word using letters only (A–Z, up to 50 characters) and press Submit Word.
  • 2. Reveal Your Global Rank: Watch your word flip into animated Wordle tiles cycling through Green, Yellow, and Gray, alongside how many Times Said and its Global Rank.
  • 3. Climb the Leaderboard: The top 50 words are ranked by global frequency with proportional share bars and medals (🥇 🥈 🥉) for the top 3. Your most recent submission is highlighted on the board!
  • 4. Live Updates: The leaderboard automatically refreshes every 30 seconds or anytime you hit Refresh.
