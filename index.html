About World Word

        

          World Word is a free
          word game built around real
          global word frequency: every player adds one English
          word to a shared pool, and the live leaderboard shows which words the
          world is saying most. If you enjoy
          daily word games, Wordle-style tile reveals, or
          comparing scores with friends, you can treat each submission as your
          daily challenge — come back tomorrow to keep your streak alive.
        

        

          Unlike a fixed “guess the word” puzzle, you choose any valid word and
          instantly see times said, global rank,
          and your share of the pool. It is a lightweight
          Wordle alternative for people who care about
          vocabulary trends, not just one hidden answer.

FAQ

        

          Is World Word like Wordle?
          

            It uses familiar green, yellow, and gray tiles for flair, but the goal
            is global frequency — not guessing one secret word. You pick any word
            and compete on rank and popularity.
          

        

        

          Is there a daily word or daily challenge?
          

            There is no single daily answer. Many players treat “one submission
            per day” as a daily challenge and track their streak on the result
            card after each play.
          

        

        

          What words or phrases are allowed?
          

            Words must pass the dictionary check (real English, letters only).
            Phrases must use real words and pass a smart sense check so random
            gibberish or empty filler wording is rejected.
